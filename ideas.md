# Design Exploration: 學習、社交及情緒適應問卷

## Three possible directions

### 1. Clinical Stationery Atelier
**Very Brief Intro:** A calm, paper-led assessment workspace where subtle rule lines, editorial typography, and measured color coding make detailed group comparison feel organised rather than overwhelming.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 2. Warm Learning Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, bookish record-keeping system that uses soft archival tones and tabbed dividers to make ongoing observations feel thoughtful and human.

**Probability:** 0.08

### 3. Clear Signal Console
**Very Brief Intro:** A compact modern control panel built around visual signal states, with a restrained digital feel intended for rapid in-session use.

**Probability:** 0.03

## Chosen direction: Clinical Stationery Atelier

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial stationery translated into a clinical documentation interface. The experience is designed as a durable working form, not a generic dashboard.

### Core Principles
1. The full form is a comparative reading surface: each item remains on the left and each child has a stable, consistently sized assessment column.
2. The interface must privilege clinical clarity over visual novelty; colour, decoration, and motion only reinforce a decision or a boundary.
3. The rating action should require one short, unambiguous gesture while remarks remain immediately adjacent to the observed item.
4. Analysis must be visibly separated from entry, allowing the teacher to move from evidence to planning without losing the original data.

### Color Philosophy
The dominant field is warm paper rather than pure white, reducing glare during detailed reading. A deep jade-teal gives the tool a calm, ownable clinical anchor. Muted amber, brick, and sage are reserved only for the three rating states so visual emphasis always carries information rather than decoration.

### Layout Paradigm
The page is structured like a working folio: a slim static identity rail, an asymmetric setup masthead, then horizontal comparison sheets with a persistent left item index. This replaces the conventional centered-card dashboard with an evidence-led reading layout.

### Signature Elements
1. A small compass-with-speech-arcs mark represents observation and guided progress.
2. Fine horizontal ledger rules and low-contrast paper texture establish a documentation surface.
3. A recurring grouped-ring motif shows individual children converging around shared goals.

### Interaction Philosophy
Controls should feel like annotations in a well-made paper form: direct, stable, and lightly tactile. The active rating gains a clear fill and border; all unselected ratings stay deliberately quiet. Planning checkboxes are used only after analysis, so selection feels consequential.

### Animation
Use short 140–200 ms opacity and transform transitions for state changes, and a restrained 180 ms press response for buttons. New child columns and the analysis panel should enter with a subtle rise from 0.98 scale and full opacity. Respect reduced-motion preferences; no looping motion is used.

### Typography System
Noto Serif TC is used sparingly for the document title and key section labels, giving the form an institutional-document quality. Noto Sans TC carries all dense clinical content with a compact but readable rhythm. Hierarchy is created with size, tone, and letter spacing rather than oversized headings.

### Brand Essence
**A precise group-observation workspace for preschool speech therapists who need to turn simultaneous observations into focused shared goals.**

Personality: composed, discerning, supportive.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are calm and declarative; CTA labels describe the action exactly; microcopy explains use without sounding promotional.

Examples: “先設定本次評估，再建立比較表。” and “整理評分結果，找出可共同訓練的目標。”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark is paired with a compact, text-free compass mark composed of three rounded speech arcs around a central observation point. The mark is large enough to identify the workspace without competing with the form.

### Signature Brand Color
**Observation Jade — #145B58**

## Style Decisions

- The working area should read as a clinical ledger surface first and an application form second; field rules, grouping, labels, and controls are treated as precise annotations on paper.
- The compass-with-speech-arcs mark is the primary identity signature wherever the workspace is introduced. It remains paired with the wordmark and should never recede into generic decoration.
- Grouped rings represent children converging toward shared goals. They recur only at major setup, comparison, and analysis transitions, rather than as arbitrary background ornament.
- The revised workflow uses a white clinical workspace with colourful, clear gradient transitions requested for energy and wayfinding. Gradients are confined to major workflow markers and never used behind assessment text.
- The document title and key workflow labels use Noto Serif TC; form content uses Noto Sans TC for small-screen clarity and dense clinical comparison.
